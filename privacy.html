Privacy Policy

Applemoor: Cozy Coloring Book. Effective October 10, 2026.

The short version

Applemoor does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores

The pages you color and your sound and vibration settings are saved only on your own device, inside the app's storage. They never leave the device unless you choose to share them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Sharing

When you tap Share, Android's share sheet opens and you decide where your picture goes, for example Drive, Messages or a printer. The picture goes straight from your device to the app you pick. We never receive it.

Children

Applemoor is made for all ages, including children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13. It contains no advertising and no links to social networks. Buying a book is a separate step that a grown-up has to confirm first.

Buying books

The first 10 pages of every book are free. A whole book can be unlocked with a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google handles the payment: we never see your card, name or email, and we receive only the fact that a book was bought, which Google Play stores for your Google account. There is no subscription. The paperback links open Amazon in your browser, only after a grown-up check.

Third parties

The only outside service the app uses is Google Play, for purchases. The app includes no third-party services that collect data. Coloring pages, pictures and sounds are all built into the app and work offline.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
